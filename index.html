What this is

We went through MenuFit the way a new user does, then rebuilt the screen where the trial is decided.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market, not inside one app. We answered every question in your iOS onboarding, from “What’s your goal?” and “When were you born?” to “Pick your favorites” and the food chips, read the Congratulations screen, then the paywall right after it, and checked it against your App Store listing and menufitapp.com.

Paywall rebuild · October 2026

The MenuFit paywall, finishing what the onboarding started

Same screen, same art direction, same “Try for $0.00”. Five changes, each fed by something MenuFit already asks, already shows or already publishes. Flip Today above the phone to see the screen as it ships.

1

Their goal and their restaurants in the headline

Today
The #1 App For Eating Out Healthy, the same for everyone, one screen after they told you what they want and where they eat.
Now
Uses: the answer to “What’s your goal?” (Cut, Bulk, Stay fit) and the restaurants from “Pick your favorites”, in the order picked. No picks: “at every restaurant”.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +15–20%to trial
2

Your rating, above the button

Today
Nothing on the paywall says how many people already use MenuFit. The only line by the button is No Payment Due Now.
Now
One strip in your card style: ★ 4.8 · 55K ratings, #14 Health & Fitness, 1.5M+ happy users, right above No Payment Due Now.
Uses: 4.8 from 55K ratings and #14 in Health & Fitness on your US App Store listing; “Join over 1.5 million happy users” from menufitapp.com.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +10–15%to trial
3

Their per-meal target, in your own bar

Today
Three questions promise to calibrate your custom calories and macros; the paywall shows no number at all.
Now
Uses: goal, birthday, activity and eating style. The bar is the “My preferences” bar from your home screen; the numbers here are a standard estimate standing in for your own formula.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +5–10%to trial
4

Their restaurant in the phone

Today
The phone shows Carbone · New York · $$$$ with a map, whatever they picked.
Now
Uses: the first pick from “Pick your favorites”, with its logo tile, and your own rows “Fat Loss Friendly Meals”, “Highest Protein Meals”, “Healthy and affordable”. The Chick-fil-A meals are the ones on your welcome screen. No map: the paywall does not need their location.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataCR +5–10%to trial
5

A second chance when they close it

Today
No close button. Restore and Try for $0.00 are the only ways forward.
Now
Uses: your Subway cutting-versus-bulking cards from the “Smart choices” screen, two lines from your App Store description, and a $14.99 MenuFit Premium price from your App Store list. No discount, no countdown.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataARPU +10–15%from people who would have left
